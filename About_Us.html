About Us

Maboho Group, a non-governmental organization established in 2020, is dedicated to advancing cancer research and improving outcomes for people affected by cancer. We work across various regions with the goal of funding research, raising awareness, and supporting patients and their families throughout their journey.

Operating in South Africa and collaborating with partners in other regions, Maboho Group focuses on supporting the efforts of cancer researchers, healthcare workers, and the well-being of the communities they serve. Comprised mainly of volunteers, medical professionals, and researchers from diverse backgrounds, our organization actively works to advance cancer research and social support.

We connect patients with the resources they need, from clinical support and treatment access to guidance for families navigating a cancer diagnosis. Additionally, we advocate for policy changes that improve access to healthcare and support services for those affected by cancer.

Aside from funding research and supporting patients directly, we raise awareness about cancer and the challenges faced by those living with it. We remain committed to scientific integrity, compassion, and building a future free from the burden of cancer.

Our Services

Cancer Research Funding
We fund research initiatives aimed at improving early detection, treatment, and outcomes for people affected by cancer.

Patient Support Programs
We provide emotional, practical, and financial support to patients and their families throughout diagnosis and treatment.

Early Detection & Screening Drives
We organize and support community screening initiatives to help catch cancer earlier, when treatment is most effective.

Access to Treatment
We help connect patients with treatment options, medical facilities, and healthcare professionals in their area.

Health and Wellness Support
We provide wellness programs and resources to support the physical and mental well-being of patients and survivors.

Educational Awareness Programs
We provide educational resources on cancer prevention, risk factors, and treatment to communities and schools.

Research Collaboration
We partner with researchers, universities, and medical institutions to advance scientific understanding of cancer.

Policy Advocacy
We work to influence healthcare policy that improves access to cancer treatment and support services.

Fundraising Events
We organize events to raise funds for cancer research, involving community members and supporters in our mission.

Volunteer Management
We recruit, train, and support volunteers — including medical professionals and researchers — who contribute their time and skills to our programs.

Caregiver Support
We offer guidance and resources to family members and caregivers supporting a loved one through cancer.

Survivorship Programs
We support cancer survivors with resources for long-term recovery, follow-up care, and reintegration into daily life.

Community Awareness Campaigns
We conduct campaigns to raise awareness about cancer, encouraging community involvement, screening, and early action.

Partnership with Healthcare Organizations
We collaborate with hospitals, clinics, and healthcare organizations to enhance the impact of our services and reach more patients.
