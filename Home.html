About Maboho Group

Maboho Group, a non-governmental organization established in 2020, is dedicated to advancing cancer research and improving outcomes for people affected by cancer. The organization operates in various regions with the goal of funding research, raising awareness, and supporting patients and their families. By working as an impartial, community-driven entity, Maboho Group creates an environment for scientific progress and compassionate care.

Operating in South Africa and collaborating with partners in other regions, Maboho Group focuses on supporting the efforts of cancer researchers, healthcare workers, and the well-being of the communities they serve. Comprised mainly of volunteers, medical professionals, and researchers from diverse backgrounds, the organization actively supports its mandate to advance cancer research and social support.

In its ongoing mission, Maboho Group remains dedicated to upholding the values of scientific integrity, compassion, and respect for patients and their families. Through its research initiatives and community programs, the organization strives to contribute to a future free from the burden of cancer. With a commitment to working alongside local communities and healthcare organizations, Maboho Group embodies a spirit of cooperation and unity in its efforts to build a healthier future for generations to come.
